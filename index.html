JB A / D move, 3 jump, 1 attack, 2 blast, 4 ultimate. Also W jump, F punch, G kick, H blast, R ultimate.
RD Left / Right move, Up jump, K punch, L kick, ; blast, apostrophe key for ultimate.
In 1 Player mode you are JB and either set of keys works. Attack chains punch, punch, kick. Blast costs half the power bar. The gold ultimate bar fills as you fight and hit. M mutes, Esc returns to the menu.
